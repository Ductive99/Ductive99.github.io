## Why this blog?

I personally believe that sharing knowledge is invaluable, and I am grateful and thankful to all the people that have shared knowledge in all of its forms. I have always enjoyed writing and explaining things, this is my way of doing so, and contributing to sharing knowledge.

## What to expect

- I like to think, and I would like to share some of these thoughts in various fields, tech and non-tech related. Thoughts, ideas and reflections I have gathered from different sources through countless hours of work.
- **NO AI**. I am not against artificial intelligence, there's no doubt that it is a revolutionary tool that has gathered much interest after the release of the LLM's. But. I want these posts to be my own, I would prefer making mistakes and would encourage anyone reading the blog post to contact me, sparking a conversation I would prefer.

Have a good day. See you in a future more interesting post.
